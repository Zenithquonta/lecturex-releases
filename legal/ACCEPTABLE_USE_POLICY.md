# Acceptable Use Policy

> **TEMPLATE requiring review by qualified counsel.** Not legal advice. Effective date: `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`. Last updated: 2026-10-14.

You must not:

1. Access or attempt to access data you are not entitled to, including bypassing row-level security, calling the backend
   API directly to read other divisions, or escalating your role.
2. Create accounts under false identity, register with another person's email, or share credentials.
3. Submit false substitution, swap, unavailability or progress records, or approve requests you are not authorised to
   approve.
4. Upload or import files that are malicious, oversized, malformed (including zip bombs and XML entity payloads),
   infringe others' rights, or contain sensitive personal data unrelated to scheduling. The app limits file types and
   sizes, but you remain responsible.
5. Probe, scan, load-test or disrupt the service, Clerk, or Supabase infrastructure, or attempt to extract the Edge
   Function secrets. Good-faith security research must follow SECURITY.md.
6. Harass, threaten or discriminate against others using free-text fields (request reasons, notes, rejection reasons).
7. Use the service for unlawful purposes or in violation of Institution policy.
8. Send repeated or bulk substitution offers, or invite again a faculty member who has already declined the same lecture,
   unless a new reason has arisen and you say so in the request.
9. Accept a substitution offer without intending to cover the lecture, or accept it to keep other faculty from being
   considered.
10. Share the details of a substitution offer (the lecture, its time, the requester or the other invited faculty) with
    anyone who does not need them for the arrangement, or outside the Institution.
11. Distribute, advertise or present a modified, re-signed, rebuilt or repackaged APK as an official LectureX release;
    distribute LectureX APK files from any source other than the official release link; or use any build of the app to
    reach the hosted service in a way this policy forbids elsewhere.

Official releases are published only at the official link in the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md#distribution-of-the-android-app).
Downloading an APK from another source is at your own risk.

Consequences: warning, request rejection, account suspension or rejection, removal of content (including a substitution
offer), and referral to the Institution or authorities. Report misuse to `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. Enforcement is currently manual
by administrators; there is no automated abuse detection (Planned).
